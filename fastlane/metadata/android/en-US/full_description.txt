Thimbly is a simple, private tracker for your sewing projects: what you are sewing, how long it takes, which pattern and machine settings you used, and how it turned out.

<b>One page per project</b>
• Status: planned, in progress, done
• Timer with one tap, or add time by hand
• Photos from the camera or gallery
• Patterns, who it is for, machine settings and notes
• When it is done: a star rating and what you learned

<b>No detours</b>
"New project" opens the project right away. Patterns, people, machines and their settings are created from the project by typing a name.

<b>Library</b>
Patterns with photos, people, and your machines with reusable settings (stitch, tensions, differential feed …), each showing the projects that used them.

<b>Backup</b>
Save or share a backup file at any time, or let Thimbly back up weekly into a folder of your choice.

<b>Private by design</b>
No internet permission, no ads, no tracking, no account. Your data stays on your phone until you export it yourself.
