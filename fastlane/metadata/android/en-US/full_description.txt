SewFar is a private diary for your sewing: what you sewed, for whom, with which pattern, what you learned, and which machine settings worked.

<b>Projects</b>
• Status from idea to planned, in progress, paused and done
• Person, patterns with the size you sewed, fabrics, notes and dates
• Photos tagged as inspiration, progress or finished, with an automatic cover
• A dated progress log with notes and photos
• When you finish: photos, lessons learned per pattern (size up, lengthen …), a star rating and notes
• "Sew again" starts a new project and shows what you learned last time

<b>Patterns</b>
Designer, category, size range, format, shop link, suited fabrics and fabric needed per size. Every project and lesson for a pattern in one place.

<b>People</b>
Who you sew for, with dated measurements, so you can see children grow.

<b>Machines & setups</b>
Your sewing machine, overlocker or coverstitch with their own setting fields. Save setups (stitch, tensions, differential feed …) per purpose and fabric, use them in projects and see when a setup has changed since.

<b>Backup</b>
Save or share a backup file at any time, or let SewFar back up weekly into a folder of your choice.

<b>Private by design</b>
No internet permission, no ads, no tracking, no account. Your data stays on your phone until you export it yourself.
